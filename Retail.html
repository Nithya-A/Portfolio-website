01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

About the Project

A business intelligence solution designed to turn retail transaction data into meaningful sales and profitability insights.

Business Problem

Retail businesses need to understand which products generate sales and profit, how customers contribute to business performance, and how sales trends change over time.

This project organizes retail data into an interactive Power BI reporting solution, enabling analysis across products, customers, dates, payments, and profitability.

  • ✦ Monitor sales performance and key business KPIs.
  • ✦ Identify product categories and products driving results.
  • ✦ Analyze customer behaviour and purchasing patterns.
  • ✦ Evaluate profit, profit margin, and sales trends.

04 / DATA ARCHITECTURE

Data Model & Relationships

A structured star-schema model supports consistent calculations, clear relationships, and flexible analytical reporting.

STAR SCHEMA

Designed for Analytical Reporting

The retail dataset was organized into a fact table and supporting dimension tables to make analysis across dates, products, customers, and payment types more manageable.

  • ✦ Fact_Sales for transaction-level analysis.
  • ✦ DimDate for date-based and time-based analysis.
  • ✦ DimProduct and DimCustomer for descriptive analysis.
  • ✦ DimPayment and a separate measure table for organization.
